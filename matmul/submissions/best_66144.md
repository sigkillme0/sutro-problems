## Summary

Same method as the 66,159 submission (see `best_66159.md`), iterated once
more. The 66,159 trace's own optimal LP duals were used to rank further
edits; the six siblings of the strongest relay split — one extra staging
copy for each of B(0,10)..B(0,15), cutting the relay lifetime just before
its final panel use around operations 8387-8403 — lower the allocation LP
from 66,159 to exactly **66,144**. (The corresponding B(1,9)..B(1,15) batch
overshoots to 66,202 and was rejected; each extra copy costs a read, and
only the B(0,*) family's tails cross remaining multiplier spikes.)

The final trace has 9,593 operations: 4,096 multiplications, 3,840
additions, 1,657 copies (the 66,199 lineage's 1,649 plus eight relay
splits), and two adjacent-swap reorderings relative to the 66,178 lineage.

The allocation is reconstructed exactly as before: IPM+crossover duals ->
rationalized interval capacity multipliers -> exact integer capacity
Lagrangian `L(y) = 66,144` (weak duality: valid for ANY nonnegative
multipliers) -> complementary slackness confines any 66,144-cost assignment
to the LP optimal face -> CP-SAT finds a face point -> per-tier optimal
interval coloring emits the physical IR. **66,144 is provably the cheapest
possible address assignment for this operation order**, including
allocations that use arbitrarily high address tiers (the top in-range tiers
carry zero multipliers, so the all-tier argmin equals the capped argmin for
every value).
