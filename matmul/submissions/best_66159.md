## Summary

The 66,178 submission proved its own operation order could never score
lower: the whole-program allocation LP is exactly 66,178 for that trace, met
with equality. This submission breaks the wall by changing the trace, guided
by the LP duals themselves.

The optimal duals concentrate their capacity multipliers into a handful of
time slots (for the 66,178-lineage order: tier-16..18 mass 21 at slot 3559, a
tier-17..25 pyramid of mass ~36 around slots 4742-4795, more at 8402). A
value whose lifetime crosses such a slot pays that multiplier in congestion.
Two kinds of one-step edits dodge individual spikes:

1. **Adjacent swaps.** Moving one instruction across a spike shifts a
   lifetime boundary by one slot. Two dependency-safe swaps
   (3558<->3559 and 3918<->3919 in the 66,179-lineage operation numbering)
   each lower the trace's allocation LP from 66,178 to 66,175.
2. **Relay lifetime splits.** One extra staging copy cuts a long B-relay
   lifetime just before its final panel use, so the expensive tail no longer
   spans the spike. Splitting the B(0,9) relay at position 8385 lowers the
   LP by 8 for one extra paid read; splitting B(1,8) at 8328 lowers it by 5.

The four edits together are exactly additive: the composed trace (9,587
operations: 4,096 muls, 3,840 adds, 1,651 copies) has allocation LP
**66,159.000 exactly**.

The allocation is then reconstructed with the same exact machinery as the
66,178 submission: event-row duals -> rationalized interval capacity
multipliers -> exact integer capacity Lagrangian `L(y) = 66,159` (a rigorous
lower bound for ANY nonnegative multipliers by weak duality) ->
complementary slackness confines any 66,159-cost assignment to the LP
optimal face -> CP-SAT finds a face point -> per-tier optimal interval
coloring emits the physical IR. The official scorer returns 66,159, and an
independent verifier reproduces all 256 outputs in the free noncommutative
polynomial ring, so correctness holds over arbitrary associative rings.

**66,159 is provably the cheapest possible address assignment for this
operation order.** The certificate chain also closes a subtle cap
assumption: because the top in-range tiers carry zero multipliers, the
per-value argmin over ALL tiers (including tiers above 27) equals the capped
argmin, so the bound holds for allocations using arbitrarily high addresses.

## Note on the search

Months of prior schedule search screened moves with an independent-prefix
floor that is provably ~220 points slack and blind to capacity-spike
structure. The decisive instruments were: a transferred-dual screen (the
optimal multipliers travel with their operations, giving a rigorous
microsecond bound per candidate edit), a window dual re-optimization screen
(median 3.5 s per candidate, exact), and full hot-started dual-simplex LP
re-solves per surviving candidate (validated to the digit against
IPM+crossover). Every number in this report is an exact-integer certificate,
not solver float output.
